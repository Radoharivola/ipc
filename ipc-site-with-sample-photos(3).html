Giving back

Our annual reforestation commitment: giving back to the soil of Madagascar

At IPC SARLU, our relationship with paper and cardboard goes far beyond the walls of our factory in Ivato. A sustainable packaging industry must give back to the environment, so every year our entire team steps away from the production floor to plant thousands of trees across the Malagasy landscape.

Our reforestation campaign (Campagne de Reboisement) unites our factory operators, design engineers and management. Together, we roll up our sleeves in the rainy season to dig, plant and nourish the soil.
