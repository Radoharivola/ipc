What we stand for

Tailor-made customization

Custom cardboard solutions for multi-colour printing requests and single-to-triple wall dimensional specs.

Sustainable eco-production

We follow FSC Chain of Custody guidelines, with environmental compliance and responsible forest management as priorities.

Waste minimization mindset

Our people know the raw material comes from responsibly managed forests, and work to cut off-cut waste when sizing custom orders.

Malagasy workforce development

We invest in local manufacturing talent and engineering training, and offer stable technical careers in Malagasy industry.

Value for money

Local businesses should never have to choose between a box they can afford and a box that protects their products.

Alignment with international norms

Ethically sourced, FSC certified materials and standardized testing protocols give you export-ready packaging that customs and global retail networks accept.

Our annual reforestation commitment: giving back to the soil of Madagascar

At IPC SARLU, our relationship with paper and cardboard goes far beyond the walls of our factory in Ivato. A sustainable packaging industry must give back to the environment, so every year our entire team steps away from the production floor to plant thousands of trees across the Malagasy landscape.

Our reforestation campaign (Campagne de Reboisement) unites our factory operators, design engineers and management. Together, we roll up our sleeves in the rainy season to dig, plant and nourish the soil.
